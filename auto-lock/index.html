FAQ

Questions parents ask

How does ChoreUnlock actually lock the apps?
It uses Apple's built-in Family Controls system. You pick exactly which apps to gate — games, video, browser, social — and they stay shielded until the day's chores are approved. Then they unlock automatically, or work on a metered XP system if you prefer. It works everywhere: home Wi-Fi, cellular, anywhere.
Can my kid uninstall it or get around it?
The shield runs at the system level, so closing the app or restarting the device doesn't lift it. No lock on any platform is beyond every determined teenager, so instead of pretending otherwise we watch for it: if the protection is turned off on a device, that device is flagged on your dashboard and your phone gets a push alert right away. You'll know within moments, not at the end of the week.
Do I need a separate device or account for my child?
No. You set it up right on your child's iPhone or iPad, and each child gets their own profile, emoji and a 4-digit PIN. Parents manage everything from the Parent Dashboard behind a parent PIN.
Is it automatic, or do I have to unlock manually?
On Premium it's fully automatic — apps lock when chores aren't done and unlock the moment they're approved. On the free plan, one child gets the full daily experience and you lock and unlock with a tap. Premium is $8.99/month or $89.99/year after a 7-day free trial.
Can I block an app permanently — like a social app?
Yes. Alongside the "earn to unlock" apps there's an always-blocked list: anything you put there stays off at all times, even after chores are done or screen time is bought.
Can I lock the whole device?
Yes. You can lock entire app groups, even "All Apps." A one-time safety step makes ChoreUnlock itself un-lockable, so the missions — and the books — are always within reach.
