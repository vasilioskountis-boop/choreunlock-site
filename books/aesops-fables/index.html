This book can earn their screen time.

Assign Aesop's Fables as a mission and pick the schedule: every day, school nights, weekends, or one fable a week. The app serves the next fable each time and keeps the bookmark.

The full text, 40 fables, on their own device. No separate book to buy or lose.
