FAQ

Questions parents ask

How does the money actually get to my child?
One tap on the ledger opens the payment in Messages, like Apple Cash, with the amount already filled in. You hit send, and the transfer happens between your accounts the way it always does. ChoreUnlock marks the week paid.
Does ChoreUnlock charge fees or hold the funds?
No. We never touch the money. ChoreUnlock keeps the ledger — what was earned, by whom, and when it was paid — and the payment itself stays between you and your child.
How is the amount decided?
You set what each chore is worth. As your child finishes missions through the week, the amounts add up on their ledger, so the total at payday is simply the work they actually did.
Is Real Rewards part of Premium?
Yes. Real Rewards comes with Premium, which is $8.99/month or $89.99/year after a 7-day free trial. The free plan still gives one child the full daily chore experience. See pricing.
Can I do both allowance and screen time?
Yes. XP can buy screen-time minutes and the week still pays out in money. Many families run both: screen time is the daily motivator, and the weekly paycheck is the long game. You choose how to run it.
