Frequently asked questions

How does ChoreUnlock work?

Parents set up daily chores and rewards. Kids complete their chores, snap a photo as proof, and earn XP, levels, and rewards. You approve each chore from your phone — and a child's screen time stays "locked" until the day's missions are done.

How do I add a child?

Open the Parent Dashboard (tap the ⚙️ gear, then enter your parent PIN) and use "Add child." Each child gets their own profile, emoji/colour, and a 4-digit PIN.

My child forgot their PIN — how do I reset it?

Go to the Parent Dashboard, open that child, and set a new 4-digit PIN. The parent always controls every child's PIN.

How does the screen-time lock work?

ChoreUnlock guides you to set up Apple's built-in Screen Time / Downtime so a child's apps or internet stay off until you approve their chores and unlock their reward time.

What does Premium cost, and how do I cancel?

Premium is a 7-day free trial, then $8.99/month (or $89.99/year — two months free), and unlocks unlimited children, AI photo checks, metered screen time, family insights, and more. To cancel, open the iPhone Settings app → tap your name → Subscriptions → ChoreUnlock → Cancel. You keep access until the end of the paid period.

What happens to the proof photos?

Proof photos are private to your family and are permanently deleted the moment you approve or reject the chore (and automatically after 7 days if not reviewed). They are never shared or used for anything else.

How do I delete my account and data?

You can delete a child, their data, or your entire account from inside the app at any time. Deleting your account removes all associated data. If you need help, email us.
