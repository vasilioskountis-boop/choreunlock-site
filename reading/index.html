How it works

Reading becomes a mission.

Pick a book, pick a child, pick a schedule. The mission walks the whole book by itself — you never manage chapters, you just watch the bookmark move.

1

Pick a book

Browse shelves by age, search by title, and check the one-line note and "minutes a night" estimate on every book.

2

Set the schedule

Every day, school nights, weekends, or one chapter a week on the day you choose. Read in order, start at any chapter, or repeat a favorite.

3

They read, then prove it

The app serves the next chapter each time. At the end it highlights a short passage and your child reads it out loud. Mak listens and checks it against the page.

4

Screen time unlocks

Pass, and the XP lands and the screen time is earned — same lock, same rules, same approval flow you already use for chores.

The honest version of reading time

No skimming. No "yes, I read it."

At the end of a chapter the app highlights a short passage and your child reads it aloud. Mak listens and checks the reading against the page. Only then does the XP land.

  • ✓The check matches the page. Mak compares what he hears to the actual passage — a made-up sentence doesn't pass.
  • ✓The bookmark is kept for them. Each time your child opens the mission, the next chapter is already waiting.
  • ✓You stay the parent, not the librarian. You assigned the book once. The app handles every night after that.
  • ✓It plugs into everything else. Reading earns XP like any mission, and XP buys screen time on the same lock and the same meter.

FAQ

Questions parents ask

Are the books really free?
Yes. All 118 books are complete, classic works included with the app — there are no in-app book purchases and never will be for the library. The books are free forever on every plan.
How does the app know my child actually read the chapter?
At the end of the chapter the app highlights a short passage and your child reads it out loud. Mak listens and checks the reading against the page. If it doesn't match, it doesn't pass — and the chapter is still there waiting.
What ages is the library for?
The shelves run from read-together picture-book age up through teens: Aesop and the Grimm tales for the little ones, the Hardy Boys, Nancy Drew and the Box-Car Children in the middle, Sherlock Holmes and Little Women for the big kids. Every book has a one-line note and a "minutes a night" estimate so you can pick the right one in seconds.
Can my child start in the middle of a book, or re-read a chapter?
Yes. When you assign a book you choose: read in order from chapter one, start at any chapter, or repeat the same chapter every time (nice for a favorite Psalm or poem). The app keeps the bookmark either way.
How does reading turn into screen time?
A reading mission earns XP exactly like a chore. On the metered plan, XP buys screen-time minutes on a countdown; on the simple plan, finishing the day's missions unlocks the apps. Either way, the lock is Apple's own Family Controls, at the system level — see how the lock works.
Do I need Premium for the Reading Library?
The library and its 118 books come with the app. ChoreUnlock is free for one child with the full daily experience; Premium ($8.99/month or $89.99/year, after a 7-day free trial) adds the whole family, the automatic lock, and the XP screen-time meter. See pricing.
